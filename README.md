## What should you look for in IPTV with subtitles?

Before choosing a plan, it helps to separate three things that often get mixed together.

**The IPTV service** provides the channel or VOD stream.

**The IPTV player** handles playback features such as the subtitle menu, audio tracks and EPG.

**The content itself** determines whether a subtitle or closed-caption track is actually available.

That last part is easy to overlook. TiviLive's current public pricing and FAQ pages list EPG, VOD, supported devices, video quality and support, but they do not explicitly promise a universal subtitle track for every channel or movie.

So, for people specifically searching for **IPTV with subtitles**, the sensible test is not simply “Does the provider support subtitles?” It is “Does the exact content I want to watch show the subtitle or CC option in my player?”

That small distinction can save a lot of head scratching.
